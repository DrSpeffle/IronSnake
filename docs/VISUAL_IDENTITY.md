# IronSnake Visual Identity

## Core Phrase

Pain is temporary.  
Pride is forever.

## Visual Tone

IronSnake should feel like:

- Streetfighter-era discipline
- Underground dojo energy
- Recovery through pressure
- Strength without polish
- Grit, sweat, scars, and pride
- RPG transformation through behaviour

## Colour Direction

Primary:
- Charcoal black
- Burnt orange
- Ember gold
- Dark steel
- Muted red

Avoid:
- Clean corporate blue
- Soft wellness pastel
- Medical app styling
- Generic fitness-app gloss

## Character

Snake is not a mascot.

Snake is the visual embodiment of:

- discipline
- survival
- rebuild
- force
- controlled aggression
- pride earned through pain
